`youtube-title` writes the titles of your long videos. Give it a topic, a
transcript or a few thumbnails and you get ranked titles. Run it after
[youtube-thumbnail](/editing/youtube-thumbnail) and you get 20 title and
thumbnail combos, with the 3 to test in A/B on top, in a preview that looks like
YouTube.

The key idea: the title never repeats the thumbnail. The thumbnail poses the
hook, the title adds the second piece, and together they tell a bigger story.

## Invoke

```bash
/youtube-title my-claude-code-test
/youtube-title 15 titles for a video comparing 3 editing tools
/youtube-title my-video thumbnails/a.jpg thumbnails/b.jpg
```

## What it does

1. **Reads the video**: transcript, chapters, notes, and the thumbnails (text,
   face, emotion, colors). With no thumbnail it writes titles alone and never
   blocks.
2. **Learns from your channel first**: your best titles with their views win
   over every other source, then a knowledge base of title levers and examples
   in French and English.
3. **Applies the rules** to every title: subject and verdict in the first five
   words, 60 to 70 characters (100 max), at most one word in caps, facts that
   are in the video only, conversational French and punchy English.
4. **Works in three rounds**: diverge (curiosity gap, emotion, number,
   contrarian, story, authority), refine (would your viewer click it, does it
   complete the thumbnail), converge (ranked, with variety).

### Two modes

- **titles**: no thumbnail batch. 10 or more titles per thumbnail (15 in all
  without one), grouped and ranked in the chat, and saved in `Titres.md`.
- **combos**: right after a thumbnail batch. Exactly 20 titles, one per
  thumbnail. It picks the A/B trio with 3 different angles (for example a funny
  fight, product and result, a confession), never 3 wordings of one idea. A
  preview opens with Home, Suggestions and Mobile views, light and dark theme,
  and buttons to copy the thumbnail and the title.

## What you need

- A thumbnail batch from [youtube-thumbnail](/editing/youtube-thumbnail) for the
  combos mode (not required for plain titles).
- Optional: a `_titles.md` with your 10 best titles and their views.
- You set up the test yourself: YouTube Studio, the video details, "Test and
  compare", up to 3 title and thumbnail variants. Nothing is changed on YouTube
  for you.
