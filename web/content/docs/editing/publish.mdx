`publish` takes a finished short and gets it out on YouTube Shorts, Instagram
Reels and TikTok. It makes the lighter export for social, picks the cover,
writes the caption and the YouTube title in your voice, shows you the full
plan, and posts only after your yes.

Without installing anything it prepares a ready-to-post kit for you to use with
each app's own scheduler. With [Treg](https://treg.to) connected to your
accounts (free), it posts for you.

## Invoke

```bash
/publish
/publish short-my-slug
/publish tomorrow 6pm, YouTube and Instagram
/publish my-short.mp4 now tiktok
```

With no argument it takes the video finished in the conversation, or the newest
one. A short that was already published is refused.

## What it does

1. **Picks the video** and checks it is the final render, never a rough cut.
2. **Makes the files**: the master for YouTube, a social export in H.264 under
   64 MB (TikTok's cap) for Instagram and TikTok, and a 1080x1920 cover taken
   from the hook (key visual settled, caption in, eyes open) and kept inside
   the central 3:4 so the profile grid does not crop it. No cover, no plan.
3. **Writes the texts** from what the short says: a caption that hooks without
   repeating the spoken hook, a TikTok caption if it should differ, a YouTube
   title of 70 characters at most, a description with your links. All of it goes
   through a humanize pass, and every name, number and claim comes from the
   video.
4. **Shows the plan** and waits: video, size, cover, platforms and accounts,
   time (local and UTC), the title and caption in full. Ask for a change and it
   shows the plan again.
5. **Posts, or hands over the kit.** Manual route: it opens the folder and gives
   you the exact clicks for each app. Treg route: YouTube is uploaded private
   and scheduled for the date, Instagram and TikTok go out now (their APIs do
   not schedule).
6. **Records it** in the short's folder so the studio pipeline moves it to
   Scheduled, then Published, with the links once live.

A short whose CTA asks for a comment goes out as three versions, one per
platform: the Instagram overlay, the same edit with the TikTok interface, and a
YouTube cut with the link on an end card.

## What you need

- A finished short with a cover (made for you if missing).
- Optional, for automatic posting: Treg installed and logged in (`treg login`),
  then `treg connections connect --provider youtube --capability post`, same for
  `instagram` and `tiktok`. Instagram needs a professional account linked to a
  Facebook Page.
- TikTok accounts not yet audited can only receive the video in their inbox:
  open TikTok within 24 hours, paste the caption and post.
