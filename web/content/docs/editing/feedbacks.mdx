`feedbacks` takes the comments you wrote in the review studio and applies them
for you. You watch the short, drop a comment or a cut at the right second, click
one button, and the edit comes back fixed with a reply under every comment.

Nothing to paste: the studio already saves every comment on disk, one file per
render, so the skill only needs to know which short you mean.

## Invoke

In the studio, click **Send to Claude**: the line `/feedbacks <file>` arrives
straight in the conversation that rendered the video. If that conversation is
closed, the button reads **Copy the feedback** and you paste the line yourself.

```bash
/feedbacks files/reviews/v2.json
/feedbacks short-my-slug
/feedbacks http://127.0.0.1:4796/short-my-slug/
/feedbacks
```

With no argument it picks the project you are already working on, or the
latest review that still has open comments.

## What it does

1. **Finds the review file.** A path, a project folder, a slug or a studio URL:
   it takes the newest version of that short and tells you which one it picked.
2. **Reads the open items.** Each comment has a time (or a range), your text,
   the words said at that moment, and sometimes the exact element you clicked
   on the edit tracks (a sound effect, a scene, the music bed). Images you
   attached are opened too: a frame you drew on shows exactly what to change.
3. **Sorts the work.** Cuts, wording, captions, timing, zooms, sounds and small
   values are applied directly in the edit. Anything that needs a new or
   reworked animated component goes to its own sub-agent, in parallel.
4. **Applies only what you asked.** The rest of the edit stays identical. When
   a comment is unclear, it takes the most likely reading and says so in the
   reply instead of asking.
5. **Closes each comment.** Every applied item is marked resolved with a
   one-sentence reply, written in your language. Your text is never changed or
   deleted. An item it could not do stays open, with the reason.
6. **Renders again** and gives you back the studio URL, with one line per item
   (done, or not done and why). It then waits for your next click on **Send to
   Claude** and runs again on its own.

If you comment an older version after a new render, the open comments are
carried over to the newest version and found again by the words they point to.

## What you need

- A short edited with [short-editing](/editing/short-editing) and its review
  studio.
- Your feedback saved from the studio (it is automatic).
