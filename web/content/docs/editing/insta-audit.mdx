`insta-audit` audits an Instagram account and gives you a sourced HTML report:
which posts over-performed, who your real competitors are, what they do that
works, and specific content experiments to try on your account.

It is evidence first. Every claim points to collected data, explanations of
virality are labeled as hypotheses, and a partial sample is never presented as
the full history.

## Invoke

```bash
/insta-audit
/insta-audit @handle
/insta-audit https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/
/insta-audit @handle 30 days
/insta-audit @handle competitors @a @b @c
```

Defaults: French, the last 90 days, up to 100 posts of the target account and
5 relevant competitors (up to 30 recent posts each). These are collection
limits, not promises of API coverage.

## What it does

1. **Resolves the account.** The handle you give, or the single Instagram
   account connected in Treg (it names it). If it is ambiguous, it asks. It
   never assumes ownership from a public search result. Niche, language and
   audience are inferred from the bio and recent posts, and labeled as
   inferences.
2. **Announces the cost, then collects through Treg.** It reads live prices and
   your balance, states the scope, keeps a per-call ledger and stops under a
   default ceiling of 1 $ (or your budget). Your own insights use the official
   connected-account endpoints when available, competitors use public ones.
3. **Finds competitors.** Candidates come from a Treg creator search by niche,
   language and audience, then each is checked against its real profile and
   recent posts. Direct peers are kept apart from larger aspirational accounts.
4. **Finds outliers.** Against each account's own baseline for the format,
   never one pooled median.
5. **Inspects the strongest posts**: hook, topic, format, duration, caption, CTA.
   It only describes audio and visuals after inspecting the media or a
   transcript (a caption is not a spoken hook).
6. **Writes the report** and verifies it: calculations against `data.json`,
   source links, missing-data labels, HTML escaping, desktop and mobile
   rendering when a browser is available.

Output, one folder per run, never overwritten:

```text
Instagram/audits/<handle>/<date-time>/
  index.html     the report
  data.json      the collected data
  sources/       sanitized API evidence
```

You get the link to the report, the strongest findings, the coverage limits and
the recorded API cost. An audit never posts, sends DMs, hosts anything publicly
or changes your account.

## What you need

- **Treg** installed and logged in (`treg login`), with a little balance.
- An Instagram account, yours (connected in Treg for insights) or any public one.
