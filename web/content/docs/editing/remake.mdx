`remake` takes the link of a reel or TikTok that went viral and prepares
everything to make your own version, with no question on the way. You get the
numbers, the reason it worked, the original script, your script (same
sentences, same order, same length, your words and your CTA), and the Recorder
open on it. You read and record.

## Invoke

```bash
/remake https://www.instagram.com/reel/<shortcode>/
/remake https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>
```

Short `vm.tiktok.com` links work too. A private account or a refused download:
save the video yourself, drop it in the reel's folder as `video.mp4`, and rerun.

## What it does

1. **Gets the data** (about 1 minute, via Treg). Views, likes, comments,
   duration, caption, the account's median, the 5 best reels of the account.
   It downloads and transcribes the video, builds a contact sheet (one frame
   every 2 s) and reads the first page of comments.
2. **Explains why it went viral**, each point with a number or a quote:
   - **Hit or luck**: x median, and whether the same concept shows up 2+ times
     above x3 on the account (a recipe) or only once (maybe luck).
   - **The hook**: the exact first sentence, its formula, who it targets.
   - **One promise**: what the hook promises and the line where it is paid.
   - **The structure** with timestamps, the visuals per beat, the CTA.
   - **What to take**: one or two things, one sentence each.

   Saved in `Inspirations/<handle>/About.md`, with a 5-line summary in chat.
3. **Writes your script.** Same sentences in the same order, same number of
   beats, same length (within 15 %), same hook formula. Only the words become
   yours (from your voice file) and the CTA becomes yours. A beat that only
   fits another country is swapped for your local equivalent and listed under
   "To check". Numbers you must provide stay `{{placeholders}}` and never reach
   the teleprompter.
4. **Saves it**: `Scripts/<slug>.md` (source and analysis, your script, the
   original verbatim with a translation, what to verify, CTA to-do) and
   `Scripts/prompter-<slug>.json` with editing notes per beat.
5. **Opens the Recorder** on the prompter file with [record](/editing/record).

A comment CTA ("Comment WORD and I send you X") needs the word in your
comment-to-DM tool. The skill writes it as a to-do with the DM text, it never
sets it up without your yes.

## What you need

- **Treg** logged in (`treg login`). A remake costs about 0.05 $.
- An **ElevenLabs key** in your profile `.env` (transcription, a few minutes of
  credits for 6 reels).
- `yt-dlp` installed (`brew install yt-dlp` or `winget install yt-dlp`).
- Ideally your brand brain (positioning, voice, offer), see
  [studio-script](/editing/studio-script). Without it, the script is written
  with what exists.
