`make-it-virale` takes one of your shorts that flopped and turns it into three
new versions, each opening on a hook that already went viral elsewhere on the
same subject. The three go out as Instagram trial reels, the numbers pick the
winner, and only the winner goes out for real.

The idea behind it: the subject is rarely the problem, the packaging is. Same
facts, same CTA, a different first sentence.

## Invoke

```bash
/make-it-virale <short slug | reel URL | dernière>
/make-it-virale results <short>
```

Flags: `--versions 3` (default), `--hooks-only` (re-record only the opening
sentences and reuse the rest of the take).

## The 6 steps

1. **Pick the flop.** Given, or the worst of your last 14 days: at least 48 h
   old, under 0.7x your median, on a subject that is still good.
2. **Why it flopped, in numbers.** Views vs your median, skip rate, average
   watch time vs length, what the first sentence and frame 0 say. Verdict:
   *packaging* (go on) or *subject* (stop, find another topic).
3. **Find the winners on the same subject.** Your own past reels that won on
   this subject first, then your saved inspirations, then Instagram search
   (Treg), keeping only reels at **x3+ their own account's
   median**. A big number on a big account is not a winner. Pull and transcribe
   4 to 7 of them, group them by hook formula.
4. **Write three versions.** Three different angles, each borrowed from a
   winner, never three wordings of one hook. Written with the `studio-script`
   rules: 5 hooks scored on its rubric, the 3 best kept, beat timing, triggers,
   quality gates and the humanize pass. Then record them.
5. **Edit the three in parallel**, one editing agent per take, each with its own
   motion and music, reviewed in the studio before anything goes out.
6. **Publish the three as trial reels**, then read the results after 24 to 48 h.
   Winner = most views **and** above your median. Share it to your profile
   (it keeps its views), push it to YouTube Shorts and TikTok, and log the
   winning formula so it becomes the first try next time.

### Trial reel settings

Trial reels are shown to non-followers only: your followers never see the same
video three times, and a losing version costs nothing.

```json
{ "settings": { "instagram": { "trial_reel": true, "graduation": "MANUAL" } } }
```

`MANUAL` graduation means you choose which one goes to your profile; Instagram
never shares one on its own.

## Results

Measured on @melvynxdev on 2026-10-21 (Instagram insights). His feed median
over the same period (24 regular reels, Oct 8 to 20): **about 4,500 views**.

### The /outliers short: flop to x3.8

The v1 did 4,711 views. Three new hooks as trial reels on Oct 19: the winner did 17,860 views, 3.8x the original and 4x the median. The full breakdown (diagnosis, winners, hooks, numbers) is in the [case study](/editing/case-study-make-it-virale).

### Other trials the same week

| short | version | views |
|---|---|---|
| Claude makes your logo | trial A | 6,310 |
| Claude makes your logo | trial B | 4,892 |
| Find Skills | hook remake trial | 209 (original: 829) |
| ManyChat replaced | trial 1 / trial 2 | 190 / 180 |

Not every remake wins. Find Skills got worse with a new hook alone, and
ManyChat never took off: for these, step 2 should have said *subject*, not
*packaging*. That is why the rule is three versions with three different angles,
and why "no version above the median" is a valid answer that means drop the
subject.

## When to use it

- A short on a good subject got under 0.7x your median.
- You have a hit formula elsewhere in your niche on that same subject.
- You want to test hooks without spamming your followers.

## Install

`make-it-virale` lives in the video-editing workspace of the Editing IA stack
(it calls `studio-script`, `record`, `short-editing`, `publish` and Treg). It is
not in a public pack yet.
