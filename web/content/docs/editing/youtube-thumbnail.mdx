`youtube-thumbnail` makes 20 original thumbnails for one long-form video. Each
one is a different click idea, in a different style, with your real face. You
pick; nothing is ever uploaded to YouTube for you.

The styles come from a library of looks that work on YouTube, plus your own
inspirations. Images are generated with Codex, free with your ChatGPT plan.

## Invoke

```bash
/youtube-thumbnail my video about Claude Code, the 30 day test
/youtube-thumbnail my-claude-code-test make a second, calmer batch
```

## What it does

1. **First run only: sets up the profile.** 1 to 5 sharp photos of you in
   `face/` (front, three-quarter, smiling, surprised, no sunglasses, nobody
   else), and a `youtube.json` with your channel and how you look on camera
   (glasses, cap, beard) so the face stays consistent. A channel without a
   face can run in faceless mode.
2. **Reads your video**: transcript, chapters, notes, or your brief. A
   thumbnail never claims what the video does not say.
3. **Writes a strategy**: what makes your thumbnails click (your own best ones
   first), then 20 click hypotheses, each with a viewer promise, a visual
   metaphor and 1 to 3 words of text in the video's language.
4. **Builds 20 concepts across the style library**: channel classics, AI
   creators, clean editorial, creator inspirations and originals, at most 2 per
   style. A course video uses a dedicated mix.
5. **Generates the 20 images** with Codex in the background (5 to 7 minutes),
   with the face, your identity line, the style reference and real logos
   injected into every prompt.
6. **Finalizes**: 1280x720 JPG files in `miniatures/`, contact sheets of 10 to
   browse, and a warning if two images are identical. The folder keeps every
   batch, with a prefix per batch.
7. **Checks each one at full size**: your real face (once), the exact text with
   its accents, real logos, no watermark, readable at phone size. A bad image is
   regenerated alone, never patched.
8. **Hands off**: the 5 best are shortlisted, then
   [youtube-title](/editing/youtube-title) writes a title per thumbnail and the
   A/B trio.

## What you need

- Codex installed and logged in with your ChatGPT account (see
  [codex-images](/editing/codex-images)).
- Your face photos, once. Optionally your best thumbnails in `_inspirations/`
  and real logos in `_logos/`.
- You set the winner up yourself in YouTube Studio ("Test and compare").
