`short-editing` is the editor. You give it a video (a file, a URL, "my last
take") and it makes the short without asking you a question: transcription, silence
cuts, scenes, captions word by word, music, sound effects, and an MP4 ready to
post. Everything follows your style profile, not a generic template.

It renders from one `spec.js` per short, so every change is a small edit and a
quick re-render, never a start over.

## Invoke

```bash
/short-editing make a short of my last video
/short-editing make a short of ~/Downloads/take-ai.mp4, keep the pricing part
/short-editing make a motion video (no camera) comparing 3 tools
/short-editing make a short of my last video, mixed with my B-roll
/short-editing studio latest
/short-editing --style punchy 1:1 <slug>
/short-editing help
```

Words go in any order: a mode (`auto`, `studio`, `help`), a visual (`motion`,
`broll`, `mix`), `--style <name>`, a format (`9:16`, `1:1`, `16:9`), a source
and a free brief. Anything the grammar covers is never asked: a missing word
takes your profile default.

## What it does

1. **Picks the visual mode** without asking: `motion` (paper, cards, counters,
   animated text), `broll` (your footage full screen where a clip matches) or
   `mix` (B-roll for moments and feelings, motion for ideas and numbers).
2. **Resolves the source:** a file, a folder, a URL (downloaded for you), `latest`
   from your A-roll, or an existing short to iterate on.
3. **Transcribes** with word-level timestamps.
4. **Plans the beats and writes the spec:** something changes on screen every 2
   to 4 seconds, the hook is readable in the first frame, a music bed is chosen
   by the emotion of the hook from a library of calm beds made in code.
5. **Builds custom components in parallel** (a real app played in its own UI, an
   illustration that acts) with sub-agents, while it writes the rest.
6. **Prepares:** cuts every micro silence, splices the voice, extracts frames.
7. **Checks stills, then fixes** against a quality bar: mobile legibility,
   captions that never cover a card, natural skin tones, discreet SFX, real logos
   for every named tool. Stills are cheap, so it iterates there, not on renders.
8. **Renders, mixes, finalizes:** ducked voice, music and automatic SFX, then a
   1080x1920 MP4 (or square, or wide) at 30 fps with a quality check.
9. **Opens the review studio**, always. You comment on the video, and the
   studio's "Send to Claude" button sends the feedback back into the
   conversation, which applies it and renders again.

### Modes

- `auto` (default): the whole edit, no questions, ends on the studio.
- `studio`: a rough cut only (silences removed, no motion), opened in the studio
  so you cut first and the edit comes after.
- `help`: prints the grammar, your styles and your newest shorts.

### Several videos at once

The `video-editing-agent` edits one video from take to MP4 in the background.
Launch one per video and they run in parallel. It never asks anything: it uses
your profile defaults and reports what it chose.

## What you need

- A style profile (run `studio-setup` once, about 10 minutes). Without it the
  engine renders with its defaults.
- An ElevenLabs key for transcribing your own takes.
- Node, ffmpeg and a Chrome or Edge for rendering (the install command handles
  them). A 90 second short renders in roughly 20 to 60 seconds on Apple Silicon.
- Optional: a B-roll library for `broll` and `mix`, Codex for generated images.
