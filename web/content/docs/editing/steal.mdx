`steal` takes a reel from a creator you admire and turns its editing into a
style you can use on any of your shorts. It does not eyeball it: it measures the
rhythm of the cuts, the layouts, the captions, the beat and the music level, the
sound effects on each cut, and the zooms frame by frame.

It then edits a test short on one of your takes, compares it tile by tile with
the original and opens it in the studio. You only see it once the gaps are
fixed.

## Invoke

```bash
/steal https://www.instagram.com/reel/<id>/
/steal ~/Downloads/reel-model.mp4
/steal copy the style of @handle
/steal this reel, call the style punchy
```

Giving two or three reels of the same format is better: one reel can be an
exception. The style is named after the creator's handle unless you say
otherwise.

## What it does

1. **Gets the video.** A local file as is, or a download from Instagram, TikTok
   or YouTube. If the download hits a login wall, it asks you to save the video
   and drop the file.
2. **Measures, in three passes** (three sub-agents in parallel when available):
   - **Frames and cuts:** contact sheets at 2 fps, layouts (full camera, split,
     cutout on color, B-roll, screenshots), caption font and entrance, stickers,
     images, hook, CTA and color grade. Key stills are saved as references.
   - **Sound:** beat tempo, how many cuts carry a click or a boom, where the
     music starts, and a spectrogram with the cuts marked. An optional voice
     isolation (it tells you first, it uses ElevenLabs credits) gives the music
     level under the voice.
   - **Zooms:** the face height range, zoom jumps per 10 seconds and the slow
     push per second. These numbers are the ceiling for your render.
3. **Writes the analysis** in your language: structure, rhythm, measured sound
   and zooms, what to keep and what to leave out (AI video clips, paid
   generation) and why.
4. **Builds the style** in your profile: colors, captions, cut rhythm, grade,
   music and beat, SFX kit, pace. Sounds come from the library that ships with
   the kit. Stickers are generated with Codex from your real photos, then cut
   out. Real logos for every app named on screen.
5. **Edits a test short** on your take with [short-editing](/editing/short-editing),
   in the original's grammar. AI action clips in the original become real
   [broll](/editing/broll) from your library.
6. **Checks against the original**, not against your house style: type size,
   sticker size, grade, no fade between shots, zoom numbers not above the
   original's, hits landing on the cuts.
7. **Delivers:** the test short opened in the studio, with what was measured, what
   was copied, what was left out, and a link. Each rejection you give is written
   in the style's notes so it is never repeated.

If the engine cannot do something the original does, it tells you what is missing
and picks the closest existing option rather than faking it in one short.

## What you need

- A reel (URL or file) and one clean take of 10 to 20 seconds in your A-roll
  folder.
- `yt-dlp` for URLs (the skill tells you how to install it).
- Codex connected to your ChatGPT account if you want stickers made from your
  photos.
- A few close-up photos of you in your profile's `face` folder.
