`studio-script` is the scriptwriter of your video studio, for shorts, Reels,
TikToks and YouTube videos. The idea: once a week you sit down for one hour,
read 5 scripts off a teleprompter and leave with every take of the week
recorded. No blank page, no motivation needed.

Every script sits on a concept that already over-performed in your niche, is
written in your voice, and goes through a pass that removes what sounds like AI.

## Invoke

```bash
/studio-script prepare my 5 shorts of the week
/studio-script 3 scripts on price objections, with the English version
/studio-script youtube <topic>
/studio-script hooks why my shorts plateau at 500 views
/studio-script rewrite my-script.md
/studio-script brain
/studio-script viral
/studio-script humanize my-description.md
```

Modes:

- no argument: the week (plan, 5 scripts, teleprompter).
- `<topic>`: one script.
- `youtube <topic>`: titles, the first 30 seconds written in full, a plan by
  chapters (the point, the proof to show, the transition), your CTA.
- `hooks`: 20 hooks from at least 10 formulas, scored, best 3 on top.
- `rewrite`: your script tightened, same facts, better hook, with a diff.
- `brain`: builds your brand brain (positioning, voice, offer, viral concepts).
- `viral`: finds the hooks that over-performed in your niche and your own feed.
- `humanize`: removes AI-sounding phrasing from any text (`humanize check` only reports).

## What it does

1. **Reads your profile and brand brain**: niche, language, CTA, then
   `positioning.md` (viewer, pains, myths), `voice.md` (how you really talk),
   `offer.md` (CTA ladder, proof), `viral.md` (concepts that worked). No profile
   yet: it offers [studio-setup](/editing/studio-setup) once, or asks 3 questions.
2. **Gathers the week's material**: your topics, your `Scripts/ideas.md`, your
   swipe file, what is hot today (trending repos, launches), and what you
   already filmed in the last 8 weeks, so no angle repeats.
3. **Writes each script**: 3 hooks from 3 different formulas out of 22, scored
   (the best is kept, below 7 out of 10 it rewrites), an on-screen hook of 6
   words or fewer, 80 to 140 spoken words, one idea, a re-hook near the middle,
   your CTA said once. Timing is checked beat by beat.
4. **Never invents facts.** Every number and result comes from you, anything
   else becomes a `{{placeholder}}` listed under "To fill".
5. **Humanizes and gates**: at least four triggers in place, then four quality
   gates (generic, structureless, interchangeable, viral). A failed gate means
   a rewrite before you see it.
6. **Saves** `Scripts/semaine-<date>/scripts.md` (the plan table and every
   script, to read and edit) and `prompter.json` (what the teleprompter reads).
7. **Hands off to [record](/editing/record)**, which opens the Recorder on the
   first script not yet shot. At the end it offers to edit all takes in the
   background, one agent per take.

A second language adds an aligned translation of every line, used later by the
translation skill.

## What you need

- A creator profile (`.short-editing/`), see [studio-setup](/editing/studio-setup).
- For `viral`: **Treg** logged in (Instagram and TikTok data) and `yt-dlp`.
- A browser with camera and microphone for the teleprompter.
