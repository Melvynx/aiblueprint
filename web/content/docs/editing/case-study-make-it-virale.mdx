## 1. The flop

The v1 went out in the feed on Oct 18 with this hook: « Ce skill cheaté m'a
permis de passer de 5 000 à 51 000 vues sur mon compte » ("This cheat skill took
me from 5,000 to 51,000 views"). At 24 h: 64 % skip rate, 9 s average watch, the
worst of his last 12 reels.

The step 1 diagnosis:

- The hook talks about the creator ("took me"), not the viewer.
- Claude only shows up at 7 s. "Cheat skill" says neither what nor for whom.
- Frame 0 shows a small view count.
- The concept sells views, an angle that tops out at 1-2x in his niche.

The subject was good, the packaging was bad: that is the case `make-it-virale`
is for.

## 2. The winners on the same subject

Step 2 searches, through Treg and the inspirations, for reels that broke out on
this exact subject (3x or more their own account's median):

- The "Claude spies on your competitors" angle: @lazymillionaire at 14.9x,
  @jayantcreates.ai at 60.2x.
- His own mould at 62x: « Tu peux maintenant transformer Claude en [métier] pour
  ton compte Instagram » ("You can now turn Claude into a [job] for your
  Instagram account").
- His `/steal` short: « Tu peux maintenant voler le style de montage de
  n'importe qui » ("You can now steal anyone's editing style").

## 3. Three hooks, three angles

| version | hook | mould |
|---|---|---|
| v2a | « Tu peux maintenant voler les vidéos qui cartonnent dans ta niche avec un seul skill Claude. » ("You can now steal the videos blowing up in your niche with one Claude skill.") | "You can now steal" (steal) |
| v2b | « Voilà comment tu transformes Claude en stratège qui espionne toute ta niche sur Instagram. » ("Here's how you turn Claude into a strategist that spies on your whole niche.") | remake of @jayantcreates.ai |
| v2c | « Tu peux maintenant transformer Claude en espion qui surveille tous tes concurrents sur Instagram. » ("You can now turn Claude into a spy that watches all your competitors.") | Claude as [job] + spy |

All three scripts passed the script rules and scored 8/10 on the hook. On paper,
there was no way to tell them apart.

## 4. The trial reel test

The three versions went out as Instagram trial reels on Oct 19, two hours apart.
A trial reel is only shown to non-followers: followers never see the same video
three times, and each hook is judged by a cold audience.

## What we learned

- **The hook decides everything.** Same video, same facts: from 308 to 17,860
  views, a 58x gap between the worst and the best trial.
- **Paper is not enough.** Three hooks at 8/10, three unrelated results. Only the
  trial told them apart, which is why the skill tests three.
- **The winner brings engagement too**: 6x the saves and 5x the shares of the
  original, not just reach.
- **What won**: "You can now" + a strong verb ("steal") + the object + Claude in
  the first sentence, over a real proof at frame 0 (the 584K-view reel it
  copies), not a promise of views.
- **Watch time does not predict the winner.** v2b had the best average watch
  (19 s) and the worst reach: it held the few viewers it got but hooked almost
  none.

## What's next

Step 6: the winner is shared to the profile from the Instagram app, then goes
out as a YouTube Short and a TikTok with `/publish`. The other two stay trials.
