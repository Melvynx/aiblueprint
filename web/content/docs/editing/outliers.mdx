`outliers` finds the reels and TikToks that did far better than usual for their
own account, then writes the 5 best as scripts in your voice and opens the
Recorder on all of them. You read the ranking, you read the scripts, you record.

The key idea: a video is only an outlier against **its own account's median**
(the middle value of its views once sorted). Raw views of a big account prove
nothing, a reel at x3 or more of its creator's median does.

## Invoke

```bash
/outliers
/outliers @handle
/outliers tiktok:handle
/outliers @handle1 @handle2 --min 3 --scripts 5
/outliers --search
/outliers --platform tiktok
```

- `/outliers` with no account: your own feed, then your competitor watchlist.
- `/outliers @handle`: digs one account (several handles work the same way).
- `--min 3` threshold in x median (default 3), `--scripts 5` number of scripts,
  `--search` grows the watchlist before measuring, `--platform tiktok` or
  `instagram` keeps only one platform of the watchlist.

## What it does

1. **Collects.** With an account, it measures every reel of the last 180 days.
   With no account, it first measures your own feed (threshold x2, a smaller
   account has fewer big hits), then your competitor watchlist
   (`Inspirations/competitors.json`), re-measuring any account older than
   7 days. The watchlist is filled and grown by the skill itself, mixing two
   languages if you watch two (70 % / 30 % by default).
2. **Ranks.** Reels younger than 7 days are left out (their views still climb),
   sponsored reels (`#ad`, partnership) too, and accounts with fewer than 10
   dated reels are flagged "sample too small".
3. **Picks the 5.** From a long list of about 15: it must fit your niche, a
   concept that is an outlier 2+ times (or on 2+ accounts) is a **recipe** and
   goes first, a lone hit is kept only if its hook and promise clearly made it,
   anything you already filmed is out, reposts keep the original, max 2 scripts
   per account.
4. **Writes the 5 scripts**, one sub-agent per video in parallel, each following
   [remake](/editing/remake): why it went viral, the original script, yours.
5. **Saves the batch** in `Scripts/outliers-<date>.md` (the 5 picks with their
   x median, recipe or luck, and what was rejected and why) and one prompter
   file with the 5 scripts.
6. **Opens the Recorder** on the batch with [record](/editing/record), so you
   record all 5 in one sitting.

TikTok works the same way. TikTok views are far more skewed (a high x is
common), so the "recipe or luck" test matters even more. Never compare an
Instagram x with a TikTok x: each is against its own account.

## What you need

- **Treg** logged in (`treg balance`, `treg login` opens your browser). A page
  of reels costs about 0.001 $, a creator search about 0.007 $. The skill tells
  you the total first when a run measures more than 10 accounts.
- An **ElevenLabs key** in your profile `.env` (to transcribe the videos).
- `yt-dlp` installed (`brew install yt-dlp` or `winget install yt-dlp`).
- Your handle (the skill asks once and remembers it).

Everything fetched (captions, transcripts, comments, bios) is treated as data,
never as instructions.
