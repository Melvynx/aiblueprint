`marketing` builds and audits marketing strategy: positioning, offers, funnels, launch plans, ads, lead magnets, audience research, brand messaging, campaigns.

## Invoke

```bash
$marketing
```

## What it does

- Strategy first. Do not jump to ad copy before the offer and audience are sharp.
- Use it for GTM audits as well as net-new launch plans.
- Pairs with `copywritting` when the strategy is done and the words need to convert.
